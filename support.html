Getting Started

How does Scandient work?

Scandient makes it easy to check if food matches your diet:

  1. Scan - Point your camera at a barcode or take a photo of the ingredients label
  2. Analyze - Our AI reads and understands the ingredients instantly
  3. Know - See if the product is vegan, vegetarian, or contains allergens

What does Scandient detect?

Scandient identifies:

  • Meat and poultry ingredients
  • Fish and seafood
  • Dairy products (milk, cheese, whey, casein, etc.)
  • Eggs and egg derivatives
  • Honey and bee products
  • Hidden animal-derived additives (gelatin, carmine, etc.)
  • Common allergens (gluten, nuts, soy)

Does it work with labels in other languages?

Yes! Scandient automatically detects the language on any food label and translates it to English before analyzing. Perfect for imported foods or when traveling abroad.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through your Apple ID settings:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Scandient
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

You'll continue to have Premium access until the end of your billing period.

Troubleshooting

The scan didn't work correctly

For best results:

  • Ensure good lighting - avoid shadows on the label
  • Hold your phone steady
  • Make sure the ingredients list is clearly visible and in focus
  • Avoid glare from shiny packaging
  • Try scanning just the ingredients section, not the entire package

Camera not working?

Make sure Scandient has camera permission:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Scroll down and tap Scandient
  3. Enable Camera access

App won't connect or load?

Scandient requires an internet connection. Try:

  • Check your Wi-Fi or cellular connection
  • Toggle Airplane mode on and off
  • Restart the app
  • Restart your device

Results seem incorrect?

While our AI is highly accurate, no system is perfect. If you find an error:

  • Try re-scanning with better lighting
  • Make sure the full ingredients list is visible
  • Report the error to us (see below) so we can improve
